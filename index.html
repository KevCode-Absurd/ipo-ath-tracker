Reading the charts

Each candle is one hour or one day of trading (Auto uses hourly for listings under 15 days old): green closed higher than it opened, red closed lower. The amber dashed line is the all-time high, the grey dotted line the IPO price, the blue line the 10-day average. The red arrow marks the bottom of the deepest dip. Drag a chart to pan; double-click the price axis to reset.

How it's measured

All-time high is the highest traded price since listing. Deepest dip is the largest fall of a daily close below the highest price reached before it. Back near high means the stock dipped at least the chosen amount and the latest price is now within the chosen band of its all-time high. 10-DMA averages the last 10 daily closes.

Data

NSE IPO calendar; candles from Upstox market data; market cap, P/E and profiles from Yahoo Finance and offer documents. BSE-only SME listings aren't covered. Refreshed weekdays after the 3:30 PM close. Charts by TradingView Lightweight Charts.

Not counted

  • Rights issues, partly-paid shares and SME migrations are left out.
